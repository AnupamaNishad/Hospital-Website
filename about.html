About Janani Hospital

Trusted Healthcare Since 2012

Established in 2012, Janani Hospital is a leading multi-specialty healthcare center dedicated to providing high-quality, affordable, and compassionate medical care. Over the past decade, we have built a reputation as a trusted healthcare partner for families in our community.

As a fully equipped multi-facility hospital, we provide comprehensive medical services under one roof. Our state-of-the-art infrastructure combined with a team of experienced doctors, compassionate nurses, and skilled support staff ensures that you receive the best care possible.

  • ✅ Advanced ICU, NICU, & Operation Theaters
  • ✅ Multi-Specialty OPD & In-Patient Wards
  • ✅ In-House Diagnostics, Lab, & 24/7 Pharmacy
